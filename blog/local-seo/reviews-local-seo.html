Reviews and Local SEO: A Practical Guide

Customer reviews can influence trust and help prospective customers evaluate a local business. A review strategy should focus on genuine feedback rather than trying to manufacture ratings.

Ask at the right moment

Make it easy for real customers to share their experience after a completed service or purchase. Provide a clear, convenient path and never pressure customers to leave a specific rating.

Respond professionally

Thank customers for positive feedback and respond calmly to concerns. Useful responses can demonstrate that a business listens and takes customer experience seriously.
