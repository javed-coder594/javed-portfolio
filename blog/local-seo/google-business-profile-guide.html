Google Business Profile Basics for Local SEO

A complete and accurate Google Business Profile can help a local business communicate essential information to customers searching nearby.

Keep the profile accurate

Use the correct business name, category, address or service area, phone number, hours and website. Add useful photos and keep important information updated when it changes.

Build trust

Encourage genuine customers to leave reviews and respond professionally. Avoid fake reviews or incentives that violate platform policies. Local SEO works best when the online profile accurately reflects the real business.
