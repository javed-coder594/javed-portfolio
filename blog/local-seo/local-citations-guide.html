Local Citations: What They Are and Why They Matter

Local citations are online references to a business and its key details. They can appear on directories, industry websites, local resources and other relevant platforms.

Consistency matters

Keep the business name, address, phone number and website accurate across important listings. Fix outdated duplicates where possible and prioritize reputable, relevant sources over submitting to every directory available.

Choose quality over quantity

Industry associations, local organizations and trusted directories can be useful. A smaller set of accurate, relevant citations is preferable to a large collection of low-quality listings.
