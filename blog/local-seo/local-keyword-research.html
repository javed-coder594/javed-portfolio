Local Keyword Research: A Practical SEO Process

Local keyword research connects services with the locations and needs customers actually search for. Start with the services a business provides, then add relevant locations and intent modifiers.

Map the customer journey

Separate discovery searches from high-intent searches such as service plus location. Consider neighborhoods, nearby landmarks and natural wording, but avoid creating thin pages for every possible location.

Turn research into useful pages

Use the findings to improve service pages, location pages, titles, headings and internal links. The page should offer genuine local value rather than repeating a location name without useful information.
