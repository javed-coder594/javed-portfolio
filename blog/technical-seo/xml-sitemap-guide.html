XML Sitemap Guide for SEO

An XML sitemap is a file that helps search engines discover important URLs on a website. It is especially useful for larger sites, new sites and websites where some pages may be difficult to discover through internal links.

What belongs in a sitemap?

Include canonical, indexable pages that you want search engines to discover. Avoid adding redirected, blocked, duplicate or clearly unimportant URLs. Keep the sitemap accurate as the site changes.

Submission and maintenance

Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and monitor whether it can be fetched successfully. A sitemap supports discovery, but it does not guarantee indexing or rankings. Strong internal linking and useful content remain important.
