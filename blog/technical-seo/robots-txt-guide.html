Robots.txt Guide: Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

The robots.txt file gives crawlers instructions about which paths they may access. It is useful for managing crawl behavior, but it is not a replacement for noindex controls or access restrictions.

Common mistakes

Be careful when blocking directories that contain important resources or pages. A poorly configured rule can prevent crawlers from accessing content you want discovered. Always test important URLs after changing the file.

Best practice

Keep robots.txt simple, document important rules and include the XML sitemap location. Remember that robots.txt controls crawling, while indexing decisions involve additional signals. Review changes as your site structure evolves.
