On-Page SEO

Internal Linking Guide for Better Site Structure

Internal links connect pages within the same website. A thoughtful internal-linking system helps visitors discover related information and helps search engines understand relationships between pages.

Build contextual connections

Link from relevant pages to important resources using descriptive, natural anchor text. A strong hierarchy can guide users from broad topics to detailed guides and back to key commercial or portfolio pages.

Avoid common problems

Check for broken links, orphan pages and excessive repetitive links. Internal links should serve a clear purpose. Review them whenever you publish a new article so older and newer content support each other.
