Keyword Research Basics for SEO

Keyword research starts with understanding what your audience needs, not simply collecting high-volume phrases. The best topics connect a real search need with a useful page.

Start with intent

Group searches by informational, commercial, navigational and transactional intent. Look at related questions, competitors and the language people use. Choose terms that match the purpose of the page you plan to create.

Build a useful content map

Map primary topics to pages and support them with closely related subtopics. Avoid forcing keywords into copy. Clear headings, helpful answers and natural language make pages easier to understand for both readers and search engines.
