Link Building Basics: A Quality-First SEO Approach

Link building works best when a link exists because another website genuinely finds a resource useful. The goal should be relevant visibility and relationships, not collecting thousands of low-quality links.

What makes a link valuable?

Relevance, editorial context and the credibility of the referring site matter more than raw backlink counts. Create resources that answer questions, provide original information or document useful projects.

Build sustainably

Use thoughtful outreach, genuine contributions and digital PR opportunities. Avoid automated spam, private link networks and manipulative schemes. Keep anchor text natural and focus on earning references that make sense to readers.
