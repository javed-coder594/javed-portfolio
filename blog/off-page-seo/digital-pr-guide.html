Digital PR for SEO: Earn Relevant Mentions

Digital PR combines useful stories and resources with outreach to journalists, publishers and communities. Strong campaigns give people a reason to mention or reference the original source.

Create something worth citing

Original research, useful tools, data summaries, expert commentary and practical guides can attract attention. Make the information easy to understand and provide clear source details.

Promote thoughtfully

Build a focused media list and personalize outreach. Avoid mass emails and exaggerated claims. A few relevant mentions can be more valuable for reputation than a large number of unrelated placements.
