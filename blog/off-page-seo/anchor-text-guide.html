Anchor Text Guide: Natural Links for SEO

Anchor text is the clickable wording used for a link. Good anchor text gives readers context about the destination without trying to manipulate rankings.

Use natural variety

Branded, descriptive and contextual phrases can all be appropriate depending on the situation. Avoid forcing exact-match keywords into every external or internal link.

Think about the reader

The best anchor text makes sense when read as part of the sentence. Review your link profile periodically, but prioritize earning useful mentions instead of engineering a specific anchor distribution.
