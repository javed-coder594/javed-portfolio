WordPress Internal Linking Guide for SEO

WordPress SEO · Written by Javed Chaudhary

Internal links connect pages within the same website. In WordPress, a thoughtful internal linking structure can help users discover related content while giving search engines clearer signals about how pages are connected.

Plan your topic structure

Start by grouping posts and pages into clear topics. Important service and category pages should have useful links from relevant supporting articles rather than being isolated.

Link from relevant content

Choose links because they help the reader take the next step. A related article, service page or guide is more useful than a link inserted only for SEO.

Use descriptive anchor text

Anchor text should describe the destination naturally. Avoid repeating the same exact phrase everywhere or using vague labels when a clearer description is possible.

Find orphan pages

Review pages that receive little or no internal linking. Adding relevant connections can make them easier to discover and improve the overall structure of the site.

Review links after publishing

New WordPress posts create opportunities to connect older pages. Make internal linking part of the publishing workflow instead of treating it as a one-time task.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many internal links should a WordPress article have?

There is no fixed number. Add links where they genuinely help readers and support the site's topic structure.

Can too many internal links hurt usability?

Yes. Excessive or irrelevant links can make content harder to read, so relevance and usefulness should guide every link.
